Terms of Service

The ground rules for working together.

These terms apply to web design and development work by thedev001 (Bappherh JR), called the Developer, for any person or business, called the Client. They apply once the Client confirms a project in writing or pays a deposit.

Scope of work

Each project starts with a written brief or quote listing the pages, features and deliverables. Anything outside it is a change request and is priced separately.

Quotes and payment

A 50% deposit is due before work starts and the balance is due before the final files are handed over or the site goes live. Prices are quoted in the currency agreed in writing. Late payment can pause work.

Timeline

Delivery dates are estimates. They depend on the Client sending content and feedback on time, and dates move if either is delayed.

Revisions

Two rounds of changes are included at each stage of design and build. Further rounds are billed at a rate agreed in advance.

Client content

The Client supplies text, images and logos, and confirms they have the right to use them. The Developer is not responsible for claims that come from materials the Client provides.

Ownership

After full payment, the Client owns the final design and code made for them. The Developer keeps the right to reuse general techniques and pre-existing code. Third-party libraries and fonts stay under their own licences. The Developer may show the finished project in a portfolio unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

Domains and hosting

Domains and hosting are registered in the Client's name wherever possible. The Client pays third-party fees, and the Developer is not responsible for outages at those providers.

Support after launch

Bugs in the delivered work are fixed free of charge for 14 days after launch. Later updates and maintenance are agreed separately.

Privacy and data

The Developer keeps the Client's private information confidential. The Client is responsible for how their own site collects and uses customer data, and for following the laws that apply to it.

Cancellation

Either side can end a project with written notice. The Client pays for work completed up to that point, and files for paid work are handed over. The deposit is non-refundable once design work has begun.

Liability

The Developer does not guarantee search rankings, traffic or sales. The Developer is not liable for lost profits or indirect losses, and total liability is limited to the amount paid for the project.

Governing law

These terms follow the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Both sides agree to try to settle any dispute in good faith before taking other steps.

Changes to these terms

The Developer may update these terms. The version in force on the day the Client confirms a project is the one that applies to that project.
