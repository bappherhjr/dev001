Terms of Service

The ground rules for working together.

These terms apply to web design and development work by thedev001 (Bappherh JR), called the Developer, for any person or business, called the Client. They apply once the Client confirms a project in writing and pays the Initial Payment.

40%Initial Payment, before work starts
40%Design Payment, when the design is approved
20%Final Payment, when everything is ready

Scope of work

Each project starts with a written brief or quote listing the pages, features, deliverables and the total price. Anything outside it is a change request and is priced separately.

Payment schedule

The total price is paid in three parts. The Initial Payment of 40% is due before any work starts. The Design Payment of 40% is due when the Client approves the design on the demo link. The Final Payment of the remaining 20% is due when everything is ready and before launch. Percentages are taken from the total price in the written quote, in the currency agreed in writing.

Demo link and design approval

After the Initial Payment, the Developer builds the project and hosts it on a private demo link for the Client to review. The Client approves the design in writing, and a message counts as writing, or asks for changes. Work on the remaining pages, content and finishing continues once the Design Payment is received.

Timeline

Delivery dates are estimates agreed in the brief. They depend on the Client sending content, feedback and payments on time, and dates move if any of these are delayed.

Revisions

Two rounds of changes are included while the design is on the demo link and before approval. Once the Client approves the design, changes to it are new work priced separately. Fixing errors in the delivered work stays free of charge.

Final check and launch

When everything is ready, the Developer tells the Client and the Client pays the Final Payment. The site then goes live on the Client's domain and the final files are handed over. Neither happens until the Final Payment is received.

Late payments

If any payment is overdue, work pauses and the delivery dates move. The Developer may take the demo link offline until the payment is made, and is not responsible for delays this causes.

Client content

The Client supplies text, images and logos, and confirms they have the right to use them. The Developer is not responsible for claims that come from materials the Client provides.

Ownership

After all three payments are made in full, the Client owns the final design and code made for them. Until then the Developer keeps ownership of the work. The Developer keeps the right to reuse general techniques and pre-existing code. Third-party libraries and fonts stay under their own licences. The Developer may show the finished project in a portfolio unless the Client asks otherwise in writing.

Domains and hosting

Domains and hosting are registered in the Client's name wherever possible. The Client pays third-party fees, and the Developer is not responsible for outages at those providers. The demo link is temporary hosting for review only. It is not for public or business use and may be removed after launch.

Support after launch

Bugs in the delivered work are fixed free of charge for 14 days after launch. Later updates and maintenance are agreed separately.

Privacy and data

The Developer keeps the Client's private information confidential. The Client is responsible for how their own site collects and uses customer data, and for following the laws that apply to it.

Cancellation and refunds

Either side can end a project with written notice. The Initial Payment is not refundable once work has begun, and the Design Payment is not refundable once the design is approved. If work done goes beyond the amounts already paid, the Client pays for it, and the Developer hands over completed work once what is due for it has been paid.

Liability

The Developer does not guarantee search rankings, traffic or sales. The Developer is not liable for lost profits or indirect losses, and total liability is limited to the amount paid for the project.

Governing law

These terms follow the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Both sides agree to try to settle any dispute in good faith before taking other steps.

Changes to these terms

The Developer may update these terms. The version in force on the day the Client confirms a project is the one that applies to that project.
